Scope: whole site (single page), mode Experience. Visitors: collectors, galleries, institutions, press, mostly on phones from a WhatsApp link.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the yard wall Cláudio paints against. Works hang on sunlit lime-washed plaster with a painted green dado band, names are sprayed through stencils. Refuses the dark-gallery masonry grid and the white-cube minimal portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Cool lime-wash wall ground, a committed plaster-green dado band carrying a third of every viewport, cool ink, one spray-yellow accent used as paint (fills, underlines, buttons). Sharp rectangular canvases with real cast shadows (offset plus blur, sun from the upper left), stencil display face, a newsroom grotesk for text.

STORY: The visitor sees the paintings first, understands the range (rostos, cidade, círculos), learns that the painter is also a poet and journalist seen on TV and in exhibitions, and shares or asks for a commission.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Stencil wordmark sprayed huge across the upper left wall; one line of subtext and a yellow stencil-plate action "Ver obras" plus a share action under it; on the right the hero portrait hung on the wall at large scale with a smaller red portrait overlapping below; the green dado band crosses the lower third behind the works.

FORM: Grounded list position 5 of 7 (the atelier yard wall), seed key f8ff3ad1. Raise from the vertical media surface: the viewer is a full-bleed vertical snap, one work per screen, caption anchored low left, actions on a right rail. Raise from the matchbook drawer: one work, one composition in the viewer.
