# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Collectors, galleries, cultural institutions, event organisers and press in Angola who discover Cláudio through a link he shares on WhatsApp and social media, mostly on a phone. They want to see the work quickly and know how to reach him.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio site for the Angolan visual artist Cláudio Arte (signature on canvas: "Cláudio Gomes Art"). Shows his paintings, his presence in exhibitions and media, and leads to commissions, acquisitions and invitations.

## Positioning
A painter who is also a poet and a professional journalist: 13 years moving between abstraction, realism and surrealism. Large-format portraits of Angolan elders, women and children, colour-block cityscapes, and experimental circular canvases with textured, poured paint.

## Operating Context
Link shared over WhatsApp (link preview with OG image matters). Viewed on mobile data in Luanda; images must be light. Hosted as a static site on Render, auto-deploys from GitHub expressers/Claudio-Arte main.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Static Vite + React. No CMS. No contact channel approved for publication yet (WhatsApp/Instagram pending the artist's consent). Artwork titles, techniques, dimensions and prices are unknown and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Cláudio Arte. Portuguese (Angola) copy. The artist's own words: "vagueio entre o abstrato, o realismo e o surrealismo"; poet; journalist; "marés da comunicação e marés plásticas".

## Evidence on Hand
public/obras: 17 paintings and artist photos (Oct 3 batch) plus Oct 4 batch: TV studio appearance with his works, studio/spray work, events, new circular works, black and white portrait. Collective projects: Banco Económico, ELA (Espaço Luanda Arte, to confirm), Instituto Camões. Career length stated as 13 and 14 years (unconfirmed which).

## Product Principles
- The work leads; the interface recedes.
- Never invent titles, prices, awards or quotes.
- Fast on a phone over mobile data.
- One clear way to reach the artist once he approves a channel.
